# One cell was adjudicated rather than read. An ink blot closed the space between
# the two letters of AT, leaving a legible Q and a stroke under the blot. The
# involution settles it: QI reads AT, and of the 676 values QI is the only one
# nothing else claims, so AT is QI and can be nothing else. The stroke carries the
# dot of this face's İ, which agrees.
#
# The other 675 entries came through on a first reading, every mirror agreeing,
# no fixed point.
