# The faintest pair of pages in the booklet.  Page 17 has no printed rule down its
# right-hand edge, so the column fit ran 12px short by column Z and clipped the last
# letter of every cell in it; XO read ME where it is MB.  The cutter now snaps its
# column positions onto the rules that are printed and carries the unruled edge out
# at the pitch those rules measure.
#
# Nothing to adjudicate once cut correctly: all 676 entries, every mirror agreeing,
# no fixed point.
